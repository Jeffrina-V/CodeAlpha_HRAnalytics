# CodeAlpha_HRAnalytics

# Human Resources Analytics Dashboard

## CodeAlpha Power BI Internship — Task 2



### Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing workforce trends,

employee attrition, job satisfaction, and performance using the

IBM HR Analytics Dataset (1,470 employees, 35 attributes).



### Dashboard Visuals

- KPI Cards: Total Employees, Attrition Count, Attrition Rate,

  Avg Age, Avg Monthly Income, Avg Job Satisfaction

- Donut Chart: Overall Employee Attrition Rate (16.1%)

- Stacked Bar: Attrition by Department

- Bar Chart: Job Satisfaction Score by Role

- Column Chart: Age Group vs Attrition Rate

- Scatter Plot: Monthly Income vs Performance Rating

- Slicers: Department, Gender, Job Role



### DAX Measures

- Total Employees, Attrition Count, Attrition Rate,

  Avg Monthly Income, Avg Age, Avg Job Satisfaction



### Tools Used

Power BI Desktop | Power Query | DAX



### Dataset

IBM HR Employee Attrition — Kaggle

1,470 employees | 35 columns | Target: Attrition (Yes/No)



### Key Insights

- Overall attrition rate is 16.1% (237 employees)

- Sales department has highest turnover

- Younger employees (18-25) leave at 38% rate

- Low income is a strong predictor of attrition
